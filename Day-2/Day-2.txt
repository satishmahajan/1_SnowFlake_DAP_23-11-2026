SnowFlake -  Stage

	- A Stage in Snowflake is a storage location used to hold files before loading them into tables or after unloading data from tables.

	- Types of stage 

		User Stage	@~	Personal user stage
		Table Stage	@%table_name	Associated with a table
		Internal Named Stage	@RAW.CSV_STAGE	Stored in Snowflake
		External Stage	S3, Azure Blob, GCS	Outside Snowflake
